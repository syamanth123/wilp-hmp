## A. Pickers — pick a person, never a pool

1. **IC → New request** (also the Library's _Create request_, which opens the same form):
   - the course's department HOG is **pre-selected**, with their load ("3 open handouts · 1 overdue");
   - choosing another HOG needs a **reason**;
   - saved as the request's HOG (`RequestAssignee` role HOG). Only that HOG is told.
2. **IC → Bulk upload of requests**: each new request gets its department's HOG. The preview shows the HOG per row;
   a row whose department has no HOG yet is an error (H13).
3. **HOG → Allocate**:
   - **PC, faculty and SME** pickers list their department's people first, with load;
   - **Show all departments** reaches anyone else (marked _other dept_);
   - the **PC is required** and is saved as the request's PC. Only that PC is told and can confirm;
   - faculty and SME are saved as today, plus an assignee row each.
4. **Change the HOG / change the PC**, with a reason: the IC changes a request's HOG; the HOG changes its PC.
   - History is kept, and the old and new person are both told.
   - This is needed now, because the assignee decides who sees a request and who is told.
   - Faculty / SME changes stay as they are today.

## C. Scoping — HOG and PC see their department

- **Rule** (one function, used everywhere):
  - a HOG / PC sees the requests of **their department**, plus any request where they are **the HOG / PC**;
  - **IC / Admin** see everything;
  - **faculty / SME**: unchanged;
  - the **Published** tab of the Library stays open to everyone.
- **Where it applies:**
  - HOG / PC lists and dashboard counts (`request-views.ts`);
  - the HOG / PC detail pages and **every HOG / PC action**, which checks the scope, not only the role. A request
    outside it answers "not found", even by URL;
  - bulk review and the other bulk tools;
  - the Library's in-progress tab, filters, counts, exports, compare and suggestions (`handout-access.ts`);
  - SLA widgets and reminders.
- **Schedules** (HOG / PC Course Dibba): their department by default, with an **All departments** switch (H14).
  These aren't confidential.
- **Department filter** in the Library and on the IC / Admin dashboards.
- The session carries the person's department, refreshed by the same 5-second check. A department change shows on
  their next page, without signing out.
- **Requests whose department isn't known** (programme not tagged), and **HOG / PC people with no department**: H9
  and H10.

## D. Existing requests get their HOG (H4)

**IC / Admin → Departments → "Give existing requests their department's HOG":**

- **Preview first:** "N requests get their HOG". The ones it can't assign are listed, each with its reason and a
  _Fix_ link:
  - department unknown;
  - department has no HOG;
  - HOG switched off.
- **Apply** writes the HOG assignee rows, audit-logged ("v1.4.1, department HOG"). It never replaces a HOG already
  set, and it can be run again after fixing the listed ones.
- PCs are not backfilled: the department's PCs see those requests through C, and the HOG picks the PC on the next
  allocation (H10).
- **Why a button, not part of the database update:** the update runs during the switch. If a HOG or a programme's
  department isn't filled in by then, it would assign nothing, and it couldn't show what it skipped. (The live site
  has 5 requests today.)

## E. "Choose a different Taxila course" (IC / Admin)

- **Where:** under the result of _Check in Taxila_, a link **Choose a different Taxila course**.
- **How:**
  - the IC types part of a name (e.g. `CIL_Exp`) and HMP searches Taxila with the **same search the HMP token
    already uses**, so IT doesn't need to grant anything new;
  - the results show name and id; the IC picks one;
  - the check runs on it at once, with the same Course Handout item rules: one `Course Handout.pdf`, any other file
    blocks, read-back after publishing.
- **Remembered per course and semester** (the course offering):
  - the next _Check in Taxila_ and every **bulk run** use the chosen course directly, by its id. This works even
    though "CIL_Exp" has no semester code in its name;
  - the panel shows "Taxila course: **CIL_Exp** — chosen by _name_ on 06 Nov 2026 · _Use the automatic match
    again_".
- **Audit and log:** choosing and clearing are audit-logged, and the publish log records whether the course was
  matched or chosen.
- **The IC's pick among several matches** is remembered the same way (H12).
- **Database:** one new table, `TaxilaCourseChoice` (course offering, Taxila course id and name, chosen by, when).

## I. As built (v1.4.1)

  (`request-views.ts`), the HOG / PC detail pages ("not found") and every HOG / PC action, bulk review (new

  (which went to every HOG / PC before).

  v1.4.1 without a PC: any PC of the department.

  `department_unknown` / `department_has_no_hog`; bulk allocate takes an optional `pc_email` column.
- **Change HOG / PC:** `lib/request-handover.ts` (reuses the hand-over from Edit person).
